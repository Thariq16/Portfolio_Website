# Imagery and iconography

## Portraits

- **Navy-background headshot** (`public/images/headshot-navy.jpg`): hero and About, in the bordered 4:5 plate. Reads as a framed portrait in both themes.
- **White-background square** (`headshot-white-square.jpg`): circular avatars (`/intro`, proposals) and the vCard photo. The standard contact-photo convention.
- Direction: studio-lit, direct eye contact, suit and white shirt, no props. Replace both together, never mix old and new portraits.

## Event and work photography

- Real photographs only: venues, slides, the actual work. Show the true subject (the Al-Ansar entrance, the real funnel slide), not stock.
- Wide figures are 16:9 with a 1px `--border` and 6px radius. One caption line in mono, sentence case, no credits inside the caption.
- Optimize to 1400-1600px wide JPEG, under about 150 KB. Descriptive filenames and real alt text on every image.
- Never reproduce another organisation's branded graphic; rebuild the facts in our own system.

## Data graphics

- Stat cards and OG images: paper ground, a hairline frame, Fraunces numerals in green, mono labels in muted ink, a faint dot grid. Three numbers at most for share images.
- Share image spec: 1200x630 JPEG, light palette, title at least 44px, readable at thumbnail size.

## Iconography

- Lucide, 1.5-2px stroke, `currentColor`. Third-party product icons keep their own colours.
- Icons support labels; they never replace them.

## Reject

Stock-photo handshakes, gradient orbs, lavender, 3D blobs, emoji as UI, anything that makes the site look like a SaaS template.
